Everything you need to learn by reading

Stories are the best way to grow your vocabulary. Storylang gives you stories at the right level, removes the dictionary and helps you remember what you learn.

Stories at your level

Every language and level comes with five ready stories, A1 to C2, and complete beginners start with First Steps: ten very short stories. They're included in the app and work offline.

Hold a word, understand it

Press and hold any word to see it in your native language, translated for the sentence it's in. It's saved to My Words automatically.

Listen and follow along

Natural narration highlights each word as it's spoken, so you hear the language while you read. Included recordings play offline.

Check your understanding

Short quizzes after each story show what you understood. You can translate words in the questions too.

Practise the words you save

Flashcards with spaced repetition bring each word back just before you'd forget it. You can practise in either direction, set daily goals and get optional reminders.

Stories about your interests

Want more? AI writes a new story at your level about whatever you're into.

Ask AI about the language

Ask about grammar, words or expressions in any language and keep going with follow-up questions.

Write and get feedback

Write a few sentences in the language you're learning. AI corrects them and explains the grammar.

Read your own files

Import EPUB, PDF, Kindle files without DRM, Word, RTF, HTML, subtitles and more. Reading them is free, and listening to them is part of Plus.

Your first story in five minutes

No account, no setup. Tell Storylang where you are and start reading.

Pick your languages

Choose the language you understand best and the language you want to learn. Translations and explanations come in your own language.

Choose your level

A1 to C2, or First Steps if you're completely new. Not sure? Start with A1 and change it whenever you like. Your stories update right away.

Read, hold, remember

Start with a five-minute story. Hold a word, see its translation and find it in My Words for practice.
